Academic Assembly in Madrid, 1884

OIL ON CANVAS • NATIONAL TREASURE ARCHIVE • MAIN HALL NORTH WING

Curatorial Commentary by Dr. Rafael Ilustre • Chief Conservator, Republic National Archive

FLAGSHIP RETROSPECTIVE • SPECIAL PRESENTATION

Dialogues Across Centuries: East & West

RUNNING EPOCH OCTOBER 15, 2024 — FEBRUARY 28, 2025

A landmark curatorial juxtaposition tracing classical European academic realism alongside the radiant pastoral impressionism of the Philippine Golden Era.

By confronting the monumental historicism of the Madrid Academy with Amorsolo’s sunlit agrarian canvases, "Dialogues" excavates how Filipino masters appropriated and transcended Western imperial visual languages, inventing an independent chromatic luminosity that redefined Southeast Asian modernism.
